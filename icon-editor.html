How it works

Draw edits straight-edged paths, rectangles, circles and ellipses. Anything else (curves, groups, transforms) is kept exactly as it is. You can still move it, recolor it, reorder it or delete it, and reshape it in Code.
  • Select: click a shape, drag it, drag its handles. Arrow keys nudge. Delete removes.
  • Pen: click points. Click the first point to close. Double-click or Enter to finish an open line. Esc cancels.
  • Rect / Circle: click and drag.
  • Change Fill, Stroke or Width with a shape selected to recolor it.
Code accepts inline SVG artwork. No scripts, no external links, no foreignObject.

Nothing reaches the live map until you Save icon, then Download Updated icons.js (or Publish) and upload it.
