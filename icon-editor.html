How it works

Draw supports straight paths, rectangles, circles, and ellipses. Curves, gradients, transforms, filters, and text are shown in the preview but not editable by the drawing tools.
  • Pen: click to place points. Click the first point to close the path.
  • Rect / Circle: click and drag.
  • Select: click a shape to select it, drag its handles to resize.
Code accepts any valid inline SVG. No scripts, no external links, no foreignObject.
