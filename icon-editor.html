How it works

Draw edits straight-edged paths, rectangles, circles and ellipses. Anything else (curves, groups, transforms) is kept exactly as it is. You can still move it, recolor it, reorder it or delete it, and reshape it in Code.
  • Select: click a shape, drag it, drag its handles. Drag on an empty spot to draw a selection box around several shapes (hold Shift to add to the selection, or to add or remove one shape by clicking it). Ctrl+A selects everything. Selected shapes move, recolor, reorder and delete together. Arrow keys nudge. Delete removes.
  • Pen: click points. Click the first point to close. Double-click or Enter to finish an open line. Esc cancels. Hold Shift to keep a line straight, flat or at 45 degrees.
  • Rect / Circle: click and drag. Hold Shift for a perfect square or circle. Without Shift, Circle makes an oval.
  • Corners: the slider rounds the corners of the next rectangle, and of every selected rectangle. A selected rectangle also has a round handle just inside its top-left corner.
  • Change Fill, Stroke or Width with shapes selected to recolor them.
Code accepts inline SVG artwork. No scripts, no external links, no foreignObject.

Nothing reaches the live map until you Save icon, then Download Updated icons.js (or Publish) and upload it.
