Tours & Trails Look

Used on tour buttons, time buttons, stop numbers and the welcome-screen button. The words on a button switch between white and dark by themselves, so they stay readable on any color you pick. Turning stops the moment a visitor touches the map, and never happens for visitors whose device is set to reduce motion.

Bar Layout (Top, Bottom, Drawer, Etc)

Click / Press and drag a button into another box. Bars run left to right and will wrap large buttons to new rows. The Bottom Bar stays hidden until you put something in it. Yellow buttons can't leave the top bar. The Legend can float over the map (as now), sit in either Bar, or go in the Drawer.

Fonts

These use fonts already on the visitor's device, so nothing extra downloads and they work offline. A device without the named font shows a similar one, so the look can differ a little between iPhone and Android.

Map Style

Only the street map changes. Aerial photos and historical map overlays keep their own colors.

Passport Look

This is only used when Passport Display is set to Progress Ring. Leave a color blank to use the Top Bar color.

Welcome Screen

{PLACE} is replaced with the place name. Use | to start a new line. The last line is the big one. The zoom options are skipped for visitors who have "reduce motion" turned on. The card never covers the top bar. Stories and Spots only appear in the two zoom options which include them, because they only show once the map is zoomed in. The point-at-places intro always uses places. A Spot with no photo is skipped.

What the welcome screen mixes in

Announcements appear while they are on, or coming soon. Only tours ticked "Show on the welcome screen" in the Tours and Trails tab. In between, the welcome screen shows places. Event tours appear through their announcement.

Allowed Area (Move the Blue Squares)

Visitors can't pan or zoom outside the blue area, so remember to leave zoom limits blank for no limit and to be generous with your boundaries.

Passport

Turn Ranks off to keep the Discovery Collection and tasks without any Stars. Which Categories can earn ranks is set per Category in the Places Editor.

Prizes

When off, the prize section is hidden from visitors and the Prizes tab in the Passport editor is disabled. Prizes are built in the Passport editor.

Sliders

Turning the opacity overlay slider off removes old map overlays from the page completely.

Event or Focus Panel

The button only appears when there is a title or text. Press Enter for a new paragraph. {PLACE} is replaced with the place name.

Phone Settings

Choose what goes in the Bottom Bar in the layout box under Buttons and Bars. Remembeer that the bottom of a phone is the easiest place to reach with one thumb.
